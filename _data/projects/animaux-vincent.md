desc_en: "As part of an exercise on the theme of animals, I took part in Vincent Boffy's project staging the 7 deadly sins through animals, and the 8th, man. We represented for example sloth through a dilapidated bedroom, gluttony in an overflowing kitchen, lust in a cage of chains, and finally man on his throne, worshipped by the 7 sins."
