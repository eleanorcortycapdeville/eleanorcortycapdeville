desc: "J'ai eu l'opportunité de travailler sur un projet dans le cadre du Nikon Festival, réalisé par Olivier Besanceney. desc_en: "I had the opportunity to work on a project as part of the Nikon Festival, directed by Olivier Besanceney. As members of the art department, we also took on the role of props assistants. We had two sets: the first, a workshop that we dressed as an art room, and the second, the young girl's bedroom, at her home, with elements referencing that same workshop."
